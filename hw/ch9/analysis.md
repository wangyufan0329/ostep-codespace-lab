## Q2 (-l 10:1,10:100)
- Prediction / 预测: While both jobs are alive, Job 0 wins a draw with probability 1/101 (about 1%). Job 1 needs 10 slices, so Job 0 runs before Job 1 finishes only if it wins at least one of the first 10 or so draws. The probability that it never runs before Job 1 finishes is (100/101)^10 = about 90.5%, so in most runs Job 1 runs its 10 slices first and Job 0 only runs afterwards (about 9.5% of the time Job 0 runs at least once earlier). In 20 seeds I expect about 18 clean runs of the form 1111111111 0000000000 and about 2 where a 0 appears earlier. / 两个作业都活着时，Job 0每次抽奖的中奖概率是1/101（约1%）。Job 1需要10个时间片，所以Job 0只有在前10次左右抽奖中至少赢一次时，才会在Job 1结束前运行。它在Job 1结束前一次都不运行的概率是 (100/101)^10 约 90.5%，所以大多数情况下Job 1先跑完10个时间片，Job 0之后才运行（约9.5%的情况下Job 0会更早运行至少一次）。20个种子里，我预计约18个是"1111111111 0000000000"这样的干净结果，约2个会更早出现0。
- Reasoning / 理由: Lottery scheduling picks a job with probability proportional to its tickets, so a job with 1 ticket out of 101 almost never wins while the other job is alive. Once Job 1 is done, Job 0 is the only job left and runs alone. Such an imbalance makes the low-ticket job wait a long time (close to starvation, although it is not impossible for it to run early), and the schedule behaves almost like "run the high-ticket job first". / 彩票调度按票数比例选择作业，所以101张票里只有1张的作业，在另一个作业活着时几乎赢不了。Job 1结束后，Job 0是唯一剩下的作业，独自运行。这样的悬殊让票少的作业等很久（接近饥饿，但它提前运行并非不可能），调度行为几乎等同于"先跑票多的作业"。
- Verified result / 验证结果: Over seeds 0-19 (`-l 10:1,10:100`), 18 of 20 runs were exactly 1111111111 0000000000 (Job 1 ran all 10 slices first, then Job 0 ran its 10 slices), and 2 runs (seeds 2 and 19) had Job 0 run once early, at the 7th slice, before Job 1 finished. So Job 0 ran before Job 1 finished in 2/20 = 10% of the runs (predicted 9.5%). All outputs are in data/q2_seeds.txt. / 在种子0-19（`-l 10:1,10:100`）中，20次运行里有18次恰好是"1111111111 0000000000"（Job 1先跑完10个时间片，然后Job 0跑它的10个），有2次（种子2和种子19）Job 0在Job 1结束前提前运行了一次，在第7个时间片。所以Job 0在Job 1结束前运行的情况是2/20 = 10%（预测为9.5%）。所有输出在 data/q2_seeds.txt。
- Analysis / 分析: The prediction matched the experiment: the one-ticket job almost never runs while the 100-ticket job is alive (90% of runs), and when it does win a draw it is rare and brief (one slice in both cases). Such an imbalance makes lottery scheduling behave almost like "run the high-ticket job first": the low-ticket job is delayed a lot but not strictly starved, because its chance of winning each draw is small but not zero (1/101 per draw). The practical consequence is that very unequal ticket allocations give the small job poor response time, while the total work is still done (all runs ended with both jobs finished after 20 slices). / 预测与实验一致：只有一张票的作业在100张票的作业活着时几乎不运行（90%的运行如此），即使它赢了一次抽奖，也是少见而短暂的（两次都只运行一个时间片）。这样的悬殊让彩票调度的行为几乎变成"先跑票多的作业"：票少的作业被推迟很久，但不是被严格饿死，因为它每次抽奖的中奖概率虽小却不为零（每次1/101）。实际影响是：票数极不均衡时，小作业的响应时间很差，但总工作量仍然会完成（所有运行都在20个时间片后两个作业都结束）。

## Q4 (-l 100:100,100:100 with larger -q)
- Prediction / 预测: As the quantum gets larger the scheduler becomes less fair (U gets smaller). Each job needs only 100/q draws, so fewer draws decide who is ahead and the luck of each draw matters more. With my own coin-flip model (not lottery.py), the average U should be about 0.944 (q=1), 0.874 (q=5), 0.825 (q=10), 0.754 (q=20), 0.626 (q=50) and exactly 0.500 (q=100, a single draw: the winner finishes at 100 and the loser at 200). / 时间片越大，调度越不公平（U越小）。每个作业只需要100/q次抽奖，决定谁领先的抽奖次数变少，每次抽奖的运气影响更大。用我自己写的抛硬币模型（不是lottery.py），平均U应约为：0.944（q=1）、0.874（q=5）、0.825（q=10）、0.754（q=20）、0.626（q=50），以及恰好0.500（q=100，只抽一次：赢家在100结束，输家在200结束）。
- Reasoning / 理由: The total time is always 200, so the second job always finishes at 200. The first job finishes at 100 + q x (draws the other job has won), and with a larger q each lost draw costs q time units instead of 1, so the first job finishes further from 200 on average. In the limit q = 100 there is only one draw. / 总时间始终是200，所以第二个作业总是在200结束。第一个作业在 100 + q x （另一个作业赢得的抽奖次数）时结束，q越大，每输一次抽奖就损失q个时间单位而不是1个，所以第一个作业平均结束得离200更远。极限情况q = 100时只有一次抽奖。
- Verified result / 验证结果: Mean U over seeds 0-19 with `-l 100:100,100:100`: q=1: 0.947 (mean first finish 189.3), q=5: 0.871, q=10: 0.848, q=20: 0.780, q=50: 0.625, q=100: 0.500 (the first job finished at exactly 100 in all 20 runs). Output is in data/q4_quantum_summary.txt. / 种子0-19、`-l 100:100,100:100` 下的平均U：q=1为0.947（第一个作业平均在189.3结束），q=5为0.871，q=10为0.848，q=20为0.780，q=50为0.625，q=100为0.500（20次运行中第一个作业都恰好在100结束）。输出在 data/q4_quantum_summary.txt。
- Analysis / 分析: The prediction was right: a larger quantum makes the lottery scheduler less fair, and U falls from about 0.95 (q=1) to exactly 0.5 (q=100, where a single draw decides everything). The measured values at q=10 and q=20 were about 0.02-0.03 above my coin-flip model, which is about one standard error for 20 seeds (about 0.022 and 0.027 in my own simulation), so this is consistent with random variation. ## Q5 (fairness graph, lottery vs stride)
- Prediction / 预测: Plotting the average unfairness U against job length (two jobs, equal tickets, quantum 1) should give a curve that starts at 0.5 for length 1 and rises towards 1 as jobs get longer. By my own coin-flip model (not lottery.py), the lottery averages should be about 0.50 (R=1), 0.63 (2), 0.75 (5), 0.82 (10), 0.88 (20), 0.92 (50), 0.94 (100), 0.96 (200), 0.98 (500), 0.98 (1000). A stride scheduler is deterministic: with equal tickets the two jobs alternate A, B, A, B, so the first finishes at 2R-1 and the second at 2R, and U = (2R-1)/(2R), i.e. 0.50, 0.75, 0.90, 0.95, 0.975, 0.99, 0.995, ... The stride curve should lie above the lottery curve at every length except R=1 (both 0.5), with the gap largest for short jobs. / 画出平均不公平度U随作业长度变化的图（两个作业、票数相同、时间片为1），应得到一条曲线：长度为1时从0.5开始，作业越长越接近1。按我自己的抛硬币模型（不是lottery.py），彩票调度的平均值应约为0.50（R=1）、0.63（2）、0.75（5）、0.82（10）、0.88（20）、0.92（50）、0.94（100）、0.96（200）、0.98（500）、0.98（1000）。步幅调度是确定性的：票数相同时两个作业严格轮流A、B、A、B，所以第一个在2R-1结束，第二个在2R结束，U = (2R-1)/(2R)，即0.50、0.75、0.90、0.95、0.975、0.99、0.995……除R=1（两者都是0.5）外，步幅曲线在每个长度上都应高于彩票曲线，作业短时差距最大。
- Reasoning / 理由: For lottery scheduling the first job finishes at R plus the number of slices the other job has already won, which is random and a bit below R, so U is below 1 but the relative gap shrinks as R grows. Stride scheduling has no randomness, so its unfairness comes only from the one-slice offset between the two jobs. Other things worth exploring: unequal ticket ratios (does the CPU share match the ticket share over a long run?), more than two jobs, the quantum size (Q4), and how much the result varies between seeds (the spread of U shrinks as jobs get longer). I did not run these extra experiments. The stride values are from theory, because lottery.py does not implement stride scheduling. / 对彩票调度，第一个作业在R加上另一个作业已赢得的时间片数时结束，这个数是随机的、略低于R，所以U小于1，但随着R增大相对差距变小。步幅调度没有随机性，它的不公平只来自两个作业之间一个时间片的错位。其他值得探索的内容：票数比例不均等（长期来看CPU份额是否与票数份额一致）、两个以上的作业、时间片大小（见Q4）、不同种子之间结果的波动（作业越长，U的波动越小）。这些额外实验我没有做。步幅的数值来自理论推导，因为lottery.py没有实现步幅调度。

- Analysis / 分析: The prediction matched. The lottery curve starts at 0.5 for length 1 and rises towards 1 as jobs get longer, and the stride curve lies above it at every length except R=1, where both are 0.5. The gap is largest for short jobs (R=2: 0.633 vs 0.75; R=5: 0.770 vs 0.90) and shrinks to under 0.02 at R=1000, so the randomness of the lottery matters mostly for short jobs. All measured values were within about 0.02 of my coin-flip model; the largest deviations (R=10: +0.023, R=50: +0.017) are within one to two standard errors for 30 seeds.
