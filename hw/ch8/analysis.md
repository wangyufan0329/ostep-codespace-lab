## Q1 (seed 0)
- Prediction / 预测: Job 0 runs 0-10 in Q1 and is demoted to Q0; Job 1 runs 10-18 in Q1 and finishes at 18; Job 0 then runs 18-25 in Q0 and finishes at 25. Response: Job 0 = 0, Job 1 = 10 (avg 5.00). Turnaround: Job 0 = 25, Job 1 = 18 (avg 21.50). / Job 0在Q1运行0-10后降到Q0；Job 1在Q1运行10-18，在18结束；之后Job 0在Q0运行18-25，在25结束。响应时间：Job 0为0，Job 1为10（平均5.00）。周转时间：Job 0为25，Job 1为18（平均21.50）。
- Reasoning / 理由: Both jobs arrive at time 0 in the top queue (quantum 10, allotment 1, no I/O). Job 0 (length 17) uses a full quantum and is demoted. Job 1 (length 8) is shorter than a quantum, so it finishes in Q1 without being demoted. Q1 is then empty, so Job 0 finishes its remaining 7 in Q0. Total time 25 = 17 + 8. / 两个作业都在时间0进入最高队列（时间片10，配额1，无I/O）。Job 0（长17）用满一个时间片后被降级。Job 1（长8）比时间片短，在Q1内跑完，不会被降级。之后Q1为空，Job 0在Q0跑完剩下的7。总时间25 = 17 + 8。
- Verified result / 验证结果: Matches the simulator output (-c): Job 1 finished at 18, Job 0 at 25; response 0 and 10 (avg 5.00); turnaround 25 and 18 (avg 21.50). Raw output is in data/q1_s0.txt. / 与模拟器 -c 的输出一致：Job 1 在 18 结束，Job 0 在 25 结束；响应时间 0 和 10（平均 5.00）；周转时间 25 和 18（平均 21.50）。原始输出在 data/q1_s0.txt。
- Analysis / 分析: The prediction was exactly right, including the assumption that Job 0 runs first (Job 1's response time of 10 confirms it). The short job (Job 1) finished at 18 even though it ran second, because the long job was demoted after one quantum; MLFQ does not know job lengths in advance but approximates shortest-job-first behavior. By hand, FIFO with Job 0 first would give average response 8.50 and turnaround 21.00, so MLFQ improves response time (5.00) while average turnaround is about the same (21.50). / 预测完全正确，包括"Job 0 先运行"的假设（Job 1 的响应时间为 10 证实了这一点）。短作业 Job 1 虽然后运行，也在 18 结束，因为长作业用完一个时间片就被降级；MLFQ 事先不知道作业长度，却近似做到了短作业优先。手算的话，FIFO 让 Job 0 先跑的平均响应时间是 8.50、平均周转时间是 21.00，所以 MLFQ 改善了响应时间（5.00），平均周转时间差不多（21.50）。

## Q3
- Prediction / 预测: Use a single queue: `-n 1 -q 10`. With jobs `-l 0,30,0:0,30,0` the order should be J0 (0-10), J1 (10-20), J0 (20-30), J1 (30-40), J0 (40-50), J1 (50-60), so J0 finishes at 50 and J1 at 60. / 只用一个队列：`-n 1 -q 10`。作业为 `-l 0,30,0:0,30,0` 时，顺序应为 J0(0-10)、J1(10-20)、J0(20-30)、J1(30-40)、J0(40-50)、J1(50-60)，J0在50结束，J1在60结束。
- Reasoning / 理由: Demotion moves a job to a lower queue; with only one queue there is nowhere to go, so all jobs stay in the same queue and take turns every quantum, which is round-robin. / 降级是把作业移到更低的队列；只有一个队列时无处可降，所有作业留在同一队列里每个时间片轮流一次，这就是轮转。
- Verified result / 验证结果: Matches the simulator output (-c): J0 finished at 50, J1 at 60; response 0 and 10 (avg 5.00); turnaround 50 and 60 (avg 55.00); execution order 0,1,0,1,0,1 with 10 consecutive ticks each. Raw output is in data/q3_rr.txt. / 与模拟器 -c 的输出一致：J0 在 50 结束，J1 在 60 结束；响应时间 0 和 10（平均 5.00）；周转时间 50 和 60（平均 55.00）；执行顺序为 0,1,0,1,0,1，每次连续 10 个 tick。原始输出在 data/q3_rr.txt。
- Analysis / 分析: The prediction was exactly right. With a single queue (`-n 1`) there is no lower queue to demote to, so the two jobs simply alternate every quantum, which is round-robin. / 预测完全正确。只用一个队列（`-n 1`）时没有更低的队列可降，两个作业每个时间片交替一次，就是轮转。

## Q4
- Prediction / 预测: `python3 ext/ostep-homework/cpu-sched-mlfq/mlfq.py -n 3 -q 100 -i 1 -S -l 0,1000,0:0,990,99 -c`. Job 0 is CPU-bound; Job 1 issues an I/O every 99 ticks (just before the 100-tick quantum ends). Over time 100 to 1100, Job 1 should get about 990 ticks and Job 0 about 9-10 ticks, i.e. about 99% of the CPU for Job 1. / Job 0是纯CPU作业；Job 1每99个tick（在100 tick的时间片结束前）发起一次I/O。在时间100到1100之间，Job 1应占约990个tick，Job 0约9-10个tick，也就是Job 1占约99%的CPU。
- Reasoning / 理由: With the old rules (`-S`), a job that gives up the CPU before its quantum ends keeps its priority and its allotment is reset, so it is never demoted. Job 0 uses a full quantum first and is demoted. After that Job 1 stays in the top queue and runs 99 of every 100 ticks; Job 0 only runs during the 1-tick I/O waits. / 使用旧规则（`-S`）时，在时间片用完前让出CPU的作业保持优先级、配额重置，所以永远不会被降级。Job 0先用完一个完整时间片被降级。之后Job 1一直留在最高队列，每100个tick运行99个；Job 0只在1个tick的I/O等待期间运行。
- Verified result / 验证结果: Matches the simulator output (-c). Over time 100 to 1100, Job 1 ran 990 ticks and Job 0 ran 10 ticks (99% vs 1%), and all 990 of Job 1's ticks were at PRIORITY 2. Raw output is in data/q4_gaming.txt. / 与模拟器 -c 的输出一致。在时间100到1100之间，Job 1运行了990个tick，Job 0运行了10个tick（99%对1%），Job 1的990个tick全部在PRIORITY 2。原始输出在 data/q4_gaming.txt。
- Analysis / 分析: The prediction was exactly right. Job 1 issues 990 / 99 = 10 I/Os, and each 1-tick I/O wait is the only time Job 0 gets the CPU, which explains the 10 ticks. This shows how the old rules (-S) can be gamed: giving up the CPU just before the quantum ends keeps a job at the top priority and lets it take 99% of the CPU. The remedy is to count the allotment across I/Os (what happens without -S, see the Fig 8.6 runs in Q2). / 预测完全正确。Job 1发起990 / 99 = 10次I/O，每次1个tick的I/O等待是Job 0唯一能用CPU的时间，这解释了10个tick。这说明旧规则（-S）可以被钻空子：在时间片结束前让出CPU，作业就一直留在最高优先级，占走99%的CPU。补救办法是把配额跨I/O累计（不加-S时就是这样，见Q2的图8.6）。

## Q5
- Prediction / 预测: Boost every 200 ms (`-B 200`): 10 ms / 200 ms = 5%. Test workload: `python3 ext/ostep-homework/cpu-sched-mlfq/mlfq.py -n 3 -q 10 -l 0,200,0:0,200,1:0,200,1 -i 1 -S -B 200 -c`. Job 0 should run about 10 ticks in each 200-tick window (0-200 and 200-400); without `-B` it should run about 10 ticks in 0-200 and about 0 in 200-400. / 每200 ms提升一次（`-B 200`）：10 ms / 200 ms = 5%。测试负载见上。Job 0在每个200 tick的窗口（0-200和200-400）里应运行约10个tick；不加 `-B` 时，它在0-200约运行10个tick，在200-400约运行0个。
- Reasoning / 理由: In the worst case the long job sits in the bottom queue and never runs while higher queues always have work. Each boost puts it back in the top queue, where it runs at least one quantum (10 ms) before being demoted again. So it gets at least 10 ms per boost period S, and 10 / S >= 5% requires S <= 200 ms. This assumes the long job gets a turn in the top queue within the period (not too many jobs ahead of it). / 最坏情况下，长作业待在最底层，只要更高的队列总有作业可运行，它就永远得不到运行。每次提升把它放回最高队列，它在那里至少运行一个时间片（10 ms）才再次被降级。所以每个提升周期S内它至少得到10 ms，而10 / S >= 5% 要求 S <= 200 ms。这个推理假设长作业在一个周期内能在最高队列里轮到一次（排在它前面的作业不太多）。
- Verified result / 验证结果: Matches the simulator output (-c). With `-B 200`, Job 0 ran 10 ticks in the window 0-200, 10 ticks in 200-400 (10 / 200 = 5%), and 180 ticks after 400. Without `-B`, it ran 10 ticks in 0-200, 0 ticks in 200-400, and 190 ticks after 400. Raw outputs are in data/q5_boost200.txt and data/q5_noboost.txt. / 与模拟器 -c 的输出一致。使用 `-B 200` 时，Job 0在0-200窗口运行10个tick，在200-400运行10个tick（10 / 200 = 5%），400之后运行180个tick。不加 `-B` 时，它在0-200运行10个tick，在200-400运行0个，400之后运行190个。原始输出在 data/q5_boost200.txt 和 data/q5_noboost.txt。
- Analysis / 分析: The prediction was exactly right. Without boost, the long job was completely starved for 200 ticks (200-400) while the two I/O-bound jobs kept the top queue busy; with a boost every 200 ms it received exactly one 10 ms quantum per period, which is the 5% floor (10 ms / 200 ms). A shorter boost period would give it a larger share, at the cost of weakening the priority separation. / 预测完全正确。不加提升时，在两个I/O密集作业一直占着最高队列的200个tick（200-400）里，长作业完全被饿住；每200 ms提升一次时，它每个周期恰好得到一个10 ms的时间片，正是5%的下限（10 ms / 200 ms）。提升周期更短会让它的份额更大，代价是削弱优先级之间的区分。

## Q6
- Prediction / 预测: `-I` puts a job that has just finished I/O at the front of its queue instead of the back, so it only matters when several jobs share a queue. Test workload (single queue so all jobs share it): `python3 ext/ostep-homework/cpu-sched-mlfq/mlfq.py -n 1 -q 10 -l 0,40,0:0,40,0:0,40,5 -i 1 -c`, with and without `-I`. I predicted that with `-I` the I/O-bound Job 2 would finish earlier. I also predicted the traces would first differ at time 35, which turned out to be wrong (see Verified result). / `-I` 让刚完成I/O的作业排在队首而不是队尾，所以只有多个作业共用一个队列时才有区别。测试负载见上（单队列让所有作业共用）。我预测加 `-I` 时I/O密集的Job 2会更早结束。我还预测两组输出在时间35第一次不同，这一点后来证明是错的（见验证结果）。
- Reasoning / 理由: Without `-I` a job returning from I/O joins the back of its queue and waits behind the other jobs; with `-I` it joins the front and runs next. For an I/O-bound job that returns often, this removes a long wait each time. My timing estimate (time 35) was wrong because I assumed Job 0 was still in the middle of its quantum when Job 2's I/O finished; the simulator decides who runs next at the moment the I/O completes. / 不加 `-I` 时，从I/O返回的作业排到队尾，要等在其他作业后面；加 `-I` 时排到队首，下一个就运行它。对经常从I/O返回的作业，这样每次都省掉一次长等待。我对时间点（35）的估计是错的，因为我假设Job 2的I/O结束时Job 0还在时间片中间；模拟器在I/O完成的那一刻就决定下一个运行谁。

- Analysis / 分析: The direction of my prediction was right: `-I` makes the I/O-bound job (Job 2) finish much earlier (67 instead of 124, almost half the turnaround time), because it runs immediately after every I/O instead of queueing behind the CPU-bound jobs. Average response time did not change because the first run of each job (times 0, 10, 20) is unaffected by `-I`. My exact timing prediction (35) was wrong: the difference shows up already at time 26, when Job 2 returns from I/O. / 我预测的方向是对的：`-I` 让I/O密集的作业（Job 2）更早结束（67而不是124，周转时间几乎减半），因为它每次I/O之后都立即运行，不用排在纯CPU作业后面。代价是两个纯CPU作业各晚结束25个tick（110和120，而不是85和95），平均周转时间只略有改善（101.33降到99.00）。平均响应时间没有变，因为每个作业第一次运行（时间0、10、20）不受 `-I` 影响。我对具体时间（35）的预测是错的：差异在Job 2从I/O返回的时间26就已经出现。
